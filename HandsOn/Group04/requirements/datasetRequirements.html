Justification of the Datasets Used

R1 – Satisfied. Both datasets belong to the environment and public health domain within a smart city context. They provide air quality monitoring data, including pollutant measurements (NO2, SO2, CO, PM10, PM2.5, O3) collected across the city of Madrid, which is directly related to environmental sustainability and citizen well-being.
R2 – Satisfied. Both datasets are available in CSV format. They use semicolons (;) as delimiters, which is a standard convention in European CSV files.
R3 – Satisfied. Both datasets are published under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license on the Madrid Open Data portal (https://datos.madrid.es), which allows their reuse and publication with proper attribution.
R4 – Satisfied.

The station dataset contains names, addresses and coordinates of real monitoring stations. The measurement dataset identifies their municipality through PROVINCIA=28 and MUNICIPIO=079. Together these values form the municipality code 28079, identifying Madrid.

The selected external linking target is the municipality of Madrid in Wikidata, with entity URI http://www.wikidata.org/entity/Q2807. Its Wikidata record identifies Madrid as a municipality and gives INE code 28079 (property P772). This provides a concrete code-based match for our local Madrid municipality entity.

During RDF conversion, the local municipality entity can be linked to this external entity using owl:sameAs. Stations will be related to the municipality through a location relation, and observations through their monitoring station. A station or observation must not be declared identical to the city. This identifies the planned links; RDF links have not yet been generated.

The two local CSV files can be joined using CODIGO_CORTO in 212629-0-estaciones-control-aire-csv.csv and ESTACION in calair_tiemporeal.csv, comparing the station codes numerically. For example, code 4 identifies the Plaza de España monitoring station. Alternatively, the station's CODIGO matches the prefix of PUNTO_MUESTREO before the first underscore: 28079004. The ESTACION column in the stations CSV contains a name, not this numeric code.

Addresses and coordinates may support additional links to streets, parks or squares after individual verification. Such places must be distinguished from the monitoring stations themselves; no unverified station-level equivalences are claimed.

R5 – Satisfied. The Madrid Open Data portal provides documentation for both datasets, including descriptions of each column and their possible values.
R6 – Partially satisfied. Both datasets come from the same data source (Madrid Open Data portal). However, they represent complementary information: one provides the location and metadata of the monitoring stations, while the other contains the actual pollutant measurements in real time. They can be cross-referenced through the station code.
